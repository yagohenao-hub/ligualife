⚙️ Methodology: Classes 1 & 2

The 4 x 15-Minute Protocol

Every individual class follows a strict 60-minute cognitive algorithm. Zero passive listening — 100% active neuromuscular production.

MINUTES 00 - 15 • BLOCK 1

⚡ Active Memory & Retrieval Sprint

Rapid-fire conversion of passive knowledge into active reflex. 10 to 12 target items fired at high velocity. Sub-3-second response constraint using interactive Flashcards.

MINUTES 15 - 30 • BLOCK 2

🧩 Native Pattern Deconstruction

Deconstructing 1 high-level structure, diplomatic nuance, or conversational transition that distinguishes a C1/C2 speaker. Eliminating literal translation habits on the spot.

MINUTES 30 - 45 • BLOCK 3

🏋️ Pushed Production Under Constraints

High-pressure scenario drills. Formulating complex arguments with strict lexical constraints (e.g. mandatory advanced connectors). Immediate recalibration if missed.

MINUTES 45 - 60 • BLOCK 4

🎙️ Free Conversational Integration & Golden Feedback

Spontaneous dialogue and professional debate. Real-time note-taking of micro-hesitations and rhythm, ending with a decisive 2-minute actionable feedback debrief.
